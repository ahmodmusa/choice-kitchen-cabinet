### Proposed Metadata
* **SEO Title:** `Tips for Cleaning Kitchen Cabinets | Grease & Care Guide` (Length: 54 chars)
* **Meta Description:** `Learn practical ways to clean kitchen cabinet grease, sticky laminate surfaces, and aluminium frames safely in Malaysian homes without causing moisture damage.` (Length: 156 chars)

### Content Outline & Structure (H2 / H3)
1. **Introduction: Why Cabinet Cleaning Differs in Malaysian Kitchens**
   * High-heat wok cooking, oil splatters, and tropical humidity create stubborn sticky grease films.
   * Why harsh chemicals, bleach, or soaked cloths permanently damage melamine edge banding.
2. **Tools & Safe Cleaning Solutions to Use**
   * Warm water + mild dishwashing liquid (the safest universal degreaser).
   * Diluted white vinegar for stainless steel handles and aluminium profiles.
   * Microfibre cloths (soft lint-free cleaning) vs. abrasive scourers to avoid.
3. **Step-by-Step Degreasing Guide by Cabinet Material**
   * *Melamine & Laminate Doors:* Wipe with damp (well-wrung) cloth, immediate dry wipe to prevent edge swelling.
   * *Aluminium Cabinets & Glass Panels:* Safe glass cleaners, streak-free polishing, hinge lubrication.
   * *Countertop Junctions:* Cleaning quartz and granite edges adjacent to base cabinet carcases.
4. **Interior Cabinet Maintenance & Pest Prevention**
   * Cleaning under sink cabinets (checking moisture traps and minor plumbing leaks).
   * Drawer liner cleaning, spice drawer spills, and ventilation to deter ants and mould.
5. **Cabinet Hardware Care (Hinges & Runners)**
   * Removing grease build-up from soft-close hinges without stripping factory lubricant.
6. **Common Cleaning Mistakes that Void Warranty / Damage Cabinets**
   * Leaving wet rags hanging over cabinet doors (swelling core boards).
   * Using strong acids, oven cleaners, or scouring pads on glossy or matte finishes.
7. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQPage Schema)**
   * *Q: How often should kitchen cabinets be deep cleaned?*
   * *Q: Can I use baking soda paste on laminate cabinets?*
   * *Q: What causes yellowing on white kitchen cabinets?*
8. **Summary & Professional Cabinet Renovation Consultation CTA**
