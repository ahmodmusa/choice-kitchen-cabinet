## 1. Selection Methodology & Pilot Philosophy

### The Pilot Rationale
Rather than attempting sweeping sitewide template changes or mass page regenerations across all 183 URLs, a **10-page pilot program** provides a controlled testing ground. This preserves existing organic traffic and rankings while validating whether substantial content differentiation resolves indexation bottlenecks and improves Search Console performance.

### Selection Criteria:
1. **Proven Commercial Search Demand:** Verified high clicks and impressions in historical Search Console data (`data-exports/Pages.csv`).
2. **Geographic Proximity & Factory Advantage:** Locations within close delivery and measurement radius of Choice Kitchen Cabinet's factory in **Puchong** (`21, Jalan Meranti Permai 6, Meranti Permai, 47120 Puchong, Selangor`).
3. **Diverse Geographic Footprint:** A balanced mix of established residential hubs in Selangor and Kuala Lumpur with high renovation activity.
4. **Diagnostic Value:** Includes high-traffic performers alongside diagnostic test cases (e.g., Kota Kemuning) to measure canonical recovery.

## 3. Practical Content Differentiation Framework for Pilot Pages

To break the 93.3% boilerplate overlap without fabricating projects or violating business reality, each pilot page will feature four distinct, individualized content modules:

```
Generic Doorway Structure (Current):
├── Hero: "Premium custom kitchen cabinets in [Area] from RM4,500"
├── 4 Boilerplate Features: "Local Expertise, Fast Installation, Competitive Pricing, 1-Year Warranty"
├── 14 Shared Stock Photos: "Recent Work in [Area]" (Same photos on 166 pages)
└── 4 Identical FAQs: "How much do cabinets cost in [Area]?"

Differentiated Pilot Structure (Proposed):
├── 1. Neighborhood Architectural Context & Common Kitchen Typologies
├── 2. Distance, Measurement & Delivery Logistics from Puchong Factory
├── 3. Transparent Material & Price Range Guidelines (Dry vs Wet Kitchens)
└── 4. Area-Specific Renovation FAQs (Condo Guidelines, Terrace Expansions)
```

### Module 1: Neighborhood Housing Profile & Layout Guidance
* **Action:** Detail the specific property types dominant in that area.
  * *Cyberjaya / Wangsa Maju:* Focus on condo layouts (800–1,200 sqft), smart space optimization, built-in microwave/fridge housing, condo management noise restrictions.
  * *Rawang / Bandar Baru Selayang:* Focus on double-storey terrace wet kitchen extensions, high-durability countertops for Asian heavy frying, quartz vs granite durability.
  * *Puchong:* Focus on immediate factory showroom proximity, custom configurations for homes in Bandar Puteri, Puchong Jaya, and Kinrara.

### Module 2: Honest Logistics & Distance Transparency
* **Current Problem:** Boilerplate claims "We have local expertise in [Area]" across 166 towns simultaneously.
* **Honest Solution:** Clearly state:
  > *"Manufactured in our central facility in Meranti Permai, Puchong, we serve [Area] ([XX] km away) with free site visits, 3D design consultations, and professional on-site installation within 2–7 days of fabrication."*
  This turns Choice Kitchen Cabinet's factory direct model into an authentic competitive advantage instead of a simulated branch office.

### Module 3: True Project Gallery Guidelines (No Fabricated Claims)
* **Strict Safety Rule:** Do not claim generic stock photos are projects installed in that town.
* **Implementation:**
  * Replace the headline *"Recent Work in [Area]"* with *"Featured Cabinet Styles & Popular Finishes"*.
  * Include clear descriptions of actual materials used (e.g., *"Matte Supermatte Acrylic with Rose Gold Edge Banding"*, *"Melamine Woodgrain Carcase with Quartz Countertop"*).
  * If authentic completed installation photos exist for that specific area, label them honestly; otherwise, present them as factory portfolio samples.

### Module 4: Tailored Local FAQs
* Replace the generic 4 questions with genuine questions relevant to local homeowners:
  * *Sample for Cyberjaya:* "Do you handle renovation permit paperwork for condominiums in Cyberjaya?"
  * *Sample for Port Klang:* "What cabinet materials hold up best against coastal humidity in Klang?"
  * *Sample for Rawang:* "Can you build cabinets for an extended wet kitchen in a new Rawang terrace house?"

## 4. Diagnostic Resolution Strategy: Kota Kemuning & Puncak Perdana

The pilot includes **Kota Kemuning** to observe how Google responds to content differentiation on a canonical-inverted URL.

### Controlled Observation Protocol:
1. **Content Overhaul on Kota Kemuning:** Apply the differentiated content framework to `/shah-alam/kota-kemuning/`.
2. **Leave Technical Tags Intact in Pilot:** Do not remove canonicals, do not add noindex, and do not create 301 redirects during the pilot.
3. **Monitor Google Search Console Inspection Tool:**
   * Track weekly crawls of `https://www.mychoicecabinet.com/shah-alam/kota-kemuning/`.
   * Observe whether Google-selected canonical transitions from `?lang=bm` back to the base URL as the base URL's content uniqueness score rises.
4. **Benchmarking Puncak Perdana:** Leave `/shah-alam/puncak-perdana/` unchanged as the control subject. If Kota Kemuning recovers canonical status while Puncak Perdana remains split with `?lang=ta`, this verifies that content differentiation directly resolves canonical inversion without risky sitewide tag changes.
